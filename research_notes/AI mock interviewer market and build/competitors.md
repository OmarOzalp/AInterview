# Competitor landscape: AI mock-interview products for tech/software candidates (as of 2026-10-30)

Method note: ~12 web searches. WebFetch failed for most hosts (interviewquery.com, codersera.com, ophyai.com not resolvable in this sandbox), so all findings are from search-result summaries, mostly vendor-affiliated comparison blogs/SEO content. Nothing below was verified on a primary pricing page unless stated. Treat prices as indicative. No direct reading of r/cscareerquestions, G2, or app-store reviews was possible.

## Which products exist, and what modality/coverage do they have?

### Takeaway
The market splits into (a) human-marketplace mocks (interviewing.io, Karat, Exponent/Pramp), (b) AI-coach/copilot consumer tools (Final Round AI, Yoodli, Big Interview, Google Interview Warmup, Huru), (c) employer-side AI interviewers (HackerRank Chakra, CodeSignal, Mercor, micro1) that are not candidate-practice products but set candidate expectations, and (d) a long tail of small AI-mock startups (MockInterviews.dev etc.). Few combine realistic voice, code execution and system design.

### Cited Findings
- interviewing.io is the most-cited product for software engineers; human mocks start around $179 per interview per a 2026 pricing breakdown; an Oct 2025 review said $225+; the AI Interviewer (coding and system design practice) and anonymous practice are reported free — [Lodely pricing breakdown](https://www.lodely.com/blog/interviewing-io-pricing); [search summary of directories](https://toolradar.com/tools/interviewing-io); [Ophy comparison](https://ophyai.com/blog/career-advice/interviewing-io-alternative)
- Pramp was acquired by Exponent (Nov 18, 2021 per Dealroom); Pramp is peer-to-peer with AI guidance; Exponent free tier reportedly gives 5 peer mock credits/month, unlimited for subscribers — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/companies/pramp); [Exponent blog](https://www.tryexponent.com/blog/pramp-joining-exponent); [Articuler comparison](https://www.articuler.ai/resources/compare/best-ai-mock-interview-tools/)
- Exponent Pro reportedly $12/mo (annual) to $79/mo (monthly) — single secondary source, unconfirmed — [IGotAnOffer Exponent alternatives](https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/tryexponent-alternatives)
- Google Interview Warmup: free, no premium tier, fixed question bank, reportedly no scored feedback — [GetHppy comparison](https://gethppy.com/talent-acquisition/ai-mock-interview-tools-comparison); [Ophy Reddit summary](https://ophyai.com/blog/interview-tips/ai-interview-assistant-reddit)
- Yoodli: speech/communication coach; free tier, premium ~$10/mo per one source (another says undisclosed); Series B $40M led by WestBridge announced Dec 2025, ~$60M total per Seedtable/hyper.ai (VCBacked shows $40M, Fundable $47M, i.e. sources conflict) — [hyper.ai](https://hyper.ai/en/stories/a14a6cf6d55526d84408646b050ecfb6); [Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/startups/Yoodli-R9Y8AK6); [VCBacked](https://www.vcbacked.co/company/yoodli); [Stork](https://www.stork.ai/en/yoodli)
- Big Interview: video-recording practice with AI feedback on eye contact, filler words, pace; $39/1 mo, $99/3 mo, $299 lifetime (July 2025 data) — [GetHppy/Capterra](https://gethppy.com/talent-acquisition/ai-mock-interview-tools-comparison); [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com.sg/software/1091862/Big-Interview). Generic behavioral, not SWE-specific.
- HackerRank "Chakra" AI interviewer: reported GA ~Oct 6 2026 after 500K+ beta interviews, consolidating three hiring rounds into one session (employer-side) — [Dealroom news](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/hackerrank-launches-chakra-ai-interviewer-after-500k-beta-tests); [bdnews24, Oct 6 2026](https://bdnews24.com/technology/lf8ktitztv). Should be verified against HackerRank primary release.
- CodeSignal: AI interviewers in credit-based plans (employer-side); AI assistant "Cosmo" for test guidance; no launch date found — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/recruiting-ats/best-hackerrank-alternatives); [Clave Prep](https://claveprep.com/blog/hackerrank-codility-codesignal-karat-coding-assessment-guide-2026)
- Karat: human-led; "NextGen" format (late 2025) embeds an AI assistant in the live session; no separate AI interviewer found — [Clave Prep](https://claveprep.com/blog/hackerrank-codility-codesignal-karat-coding-assessment-guide-2026)
- Mercor: AI interviewer "Monty" screens experts for AI labs; Series C $350M at $10B (Oct 2025); reports of $14.19B (Jul 2026) and talks at $20B are less certain — [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/private/mercor/); [Second Talent](https://www.secondtalent.com/resources/top-ai-recruiting-startups-usa/). micro1: AI recruiter "Zara" runs 20-30 min technical interviews; $35M at $500M (Sep 2025); Forbes via search summary: >$100M ARR Feb 2026; a $4B valuation claim is undated/unconfirmed — [InforCapital](https://inforcapital.com/news/ai-training-data-startup-micro1-reportedly-hits-4b-valuation/); [texxr](https://www.texxr.com/entity/micro1). Both are employer/marketplace products, not candidate practice.
- Other names in 2026 roundups: MockInterviews.dev (AI, SWE coding + system design, rated 4.5 by one guide only), Nora AI, Huru (aimed at non-technical/budget users; no primary data found), LockedIn AI (live-interview copilot) — [Fast.io](https://fast.io/resources/best-ai-interview-prep-tools-2026/); [Interview Query roundup](https://www.interviewquery.com/p/best-ai-interviewer-platforms)
- Apriora, Alex, Interview Kickstart, BrainRider: not researched (no results retrieved).

### Inferences
- The only free, AI-driven, SWE-specific coding + system design option surfaced is interviewing.io's AI Interviewer and small startups; big prep brands are behavioral/generic.
- Employer-side AI interviewers (Chakra, Monty, Zara) will raise candidate demand for realistic AI-interview practice; they are not competitors in practice but are the format candidates will face.

### Gaps
- Voice vs text per product not verified on primary pages. Apriora, Alex, Interview Kickstart, BrainRider, Huru unresearched/no data. Exponent, Final Round, interviewing.io pricing pages not read directly.

## Pricing, funding, traction

### Takeaway
Pricing spans free (Google, interviewing.io AI) to ~$150/mo (Final Round AI month-to-month) and $179+/human session. Only Final Round (seed), Yoodli (Series B), Mercor/micro1 have clear funding data.

### Cited Findings
- Final Round AI: $6.88M seed, Jan 2025, led by Uncork Capital; no later round found — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/final-round-ai-job-search-journey-company-closes-6-88-million-seed/amp/); [VCBacked](https://www.vcbacked.co/company/final-round-ai)
- Final Round AI pricing reported $150/mo, $250 quarterly, $300/yr, no free trial (checked 2026-09-29 by a competitor, OphyAI; verify) — [OphyAI](https://ophyai.com/blog/career-advice/final-round-ai-pricing). A reviewer cited a $249.99 three-month plan — [Trustpilot](https://nl-be.trustpilot.com/review/finalroundai.com)
- Paid AI prep tools typically ~$9-30/mo; one guide says free tiers have shrunk in the past 12 months — search summary of [Fast.io](https://fast.io/resources/best-ai-interview-prep-tools-2026/)
- interviewing.io, Pramp/Exponent, Karat, Big Interview funding/user counts: not found.

### Inferences
- A large gap between expensive human mocks (~$179+) and $10-30 AI tools leaves room for a mid-priced, high-fidelity AI product; Final Round's price is high relative to peers.

### Gaps
- User counts/revenue for nearly all consumer products. Yoodli total funding conflicts between sources.

## User complaints and quality shortfalls

### Takeaway
Evidence is thin and secondhand. Clearest signals: billing/refund disputes (Final Round AI), lag in live copilots (LockedIn), and no scored feedback / fixed questions (Google Warmup). No primary Reddit/G2/app-review data obtained.

### Cited Findings
- Final Round AI Trustpilot: ratings reported inconsistently, 2.8 (~280 reviews, ~28% 1-star), 3.5 (localized page), 3.9 (~255); complaints: refunds refused, unexpected annual charges, AI-only support; Product Hunt ~4.9 from 72 (OphyAI, competitor) — [Weekday](https://www.weekday.works/post/final-round-ai-review); [Trustpilot](https://fr-be.trustpilot.com/review/finalroundai.com); [Interview Coder, Jun 2026](https://www.interviewcoder.co/blog/final-round-ai-review); [OphyAI](https://ophyai.com/blog/career-advice/final-round-ai-pricing)
- Vendor blog summaries of Reddit: mock-interview/practice tools draw little pushback; controversy centers on live assistants (detectability, stiff delivery, community disapproval in no-tool coding OAs); LockedIn complaints: response lag, opaque pricing — [AceRound](https://www.aceround.app/blog/ai-interview-assistant-reddit/); [OphyAI](https://ophyai.com/blog/interview-tips/ai-interview-assistant-reddit); [LockedIn blog](https://www.lockedinai.com/blog/lockedin-ai-reddit-reviews). All competitor-authored.
- Exponent/Pramp: reviews say upgrades pushed harder post-acquisition, peer availability limited (unconfirmed) — [Articuler](https://www.articuler.ai/resources/compare/best-ai-mock-interview-tools/)

### Inferences
- (Inference, not evidence) Likely shortfalls for AI mocks: shallow follow-ups, no real code execution/debugging context, voice latency, generic feedback. Not substantiated by sources here and should be validated with primary user research.
